`massive-fr-FR-test-200.csv`: 200 utterances from the fr-FR `test` partition of
[MASSIVE 1.1](https://github.com/alexa/massive) (Amazon), shuffled with seed 42, columns
`utt` and `intent` only. Licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

FitzGerald et al., "MASSIVE: A 1M-Example Multilingual Natural Language Understanding Dataset
with 51 Typologically-Diverse Languages", 2022.
